⚠️ Honesty — what actually leaves your machine
  • Phase A and Phase B: nothing leaves. Ever. No cloud.
  • Phase C with a local model: nothing leaves there either.
  • Phase C with a paid model: species names only, and only after you said yes.
  • The cost cannot be predicted up front: it starts at 5 species so you can measure it, then you decide.
  • The dossier is written by an LLM, not by an expert: that is stated at the top of it, and citations are re-checked against NCBI.
The LLM is the guide: it interviews the user, runs the scripts and interprets the results.
It never improvises commands: it only executes the tested scripts shipped in the repository (bin/ and steps/).
